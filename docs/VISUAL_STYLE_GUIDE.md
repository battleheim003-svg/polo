# Chogan Visual Style Guide

## Palette

- Deep Lapis `#0A1C3A`: HUD panels, skyline, pause surfaces.
- Turquoise `#08A1AD`: player identity, aim energy, active touch highlights.
- Warm Sand `#B38F5C`: outer ground, dust, environmental base.
- Parchment `#E8D6A3`: field lines, ball fill, readable text.
- Burgundy `#7D151F`: future opponent identity, strike control, accent banners.
- Burnt Sienna `#8C4521`: rails, saddle, warm architectural accents.
- Muted Gold `#DBA83F`: selection ring, goal feedback, thin panel borders.
- Dark Ink `#0A0A0D`: outlines, mallet, ball rim, high contrast details.

## Scale And Line Rules

- Rider cutout target: 82 by 58 world-scaled pixels at 1280x720.
- Ball target: 24-34 world-scaled pixels with dark outline and shadow.
- Main field lines: 2-4 px, parchment at partial opacity.
- Goal posts: 7-8 px, parchment/gold flash on score.
- Aim path: short segmented strokes, 5 px, turquoise/gold.

## Shadow Style

Use soft oval shadows under rider and ball. Shadows should anchor objects to the field without reading as separate gameplay zones.

## Panel Style

HUD panels use Deep Lapis with low opacity, thin Muted Gold borders, Parchment text, and compact labels. Event text appears as a temporary toast, not permanent header text.

## Icon Style

Use simple text/icon hybrids only for temporary foundation UI. Keep icon marks high-contrast, single-color, and aligned with the palette.

## Team Rules

- Player: turquoise/lapis cloth with gold selection.
- Opponent/future target: burgundy/ochre.
- Horses: brown, gray, or pale neutral with darker legs and head detail.

## Ball Readability

The ball must keep a parchment/cream fill, Dark Ink outline, independent shadow, and a gold trail only while fast. It should remain readable on field lines.

## Field Composition

The field should read as one continuous match ground: textured green play area, sand perimeter, center line/circle, attack/defense arcs, goal structures, small flags, spectator/architecture band, and limited Persian geometric border motifs. Domain zones may inform placement but must not look like five separate test rectangles.
